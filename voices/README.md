# Your own voices (optional)

With `FV_TTS_MODEL=Qwen/Qwen3-TTS-12Hz-1.7B-Base` the TTS service clones voices from recordings in this folder:

- `name.wav` – 10–30 s of clean speech (one speaker, no music, little room echo)
- `name.txt` – exactly what is said in it

Only use voices of people who agreed to it. The files in this folder are not committed (see `.gitignore`).
